Stethoscope

Listen to a machine, keep its baseline, and see what changed. It does not diagnose.

about

Record a machine

There is no microphone here: the app works on a sample buffer it can hold and test. Pick a machine and record what it sounds like.

Against the baseline

A band-by-band comparison with the machine's own first recording. A low score means it sounds different from how it sounded before, and nothing more.

    The trend

      Stethoscope compares a machine's recording with its own baseline and names the band that stands out. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and a low score is not a verdict on the machine.
