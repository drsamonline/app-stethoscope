Stethoscope

Listen to a machine, keep its baseline, and see what changed — with the finding named and its frequency shown.

Open Stethoscope

This is a recording of a machine compared with its own baseline. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and a low score is not a verdict on the machine.

Status Built and working: a spectrum from a sample buffer; a baseline per machine; a score against that baseline; findings that name the band and quote the frequency; a trend across recordings; and an export.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: a microphone. The app works on a sample buffer it can hold and test, and the page says so — a microphone would be a second job and a privacy question the spec does not ask for.

What it does

  • Takes a sample buffer, computes a small spectrum, and keeps the first recording as the machine's baseline.
  • Scores a later recording against that baseline, band by band.
  • Names what stands out — a low grind, a mid whine, a high whistle — with the frequency in hertz.
  • Plots a trend across recordings.

What it will not do

  • Diagnose. No condition, fault or verdict field exists in the engine.
  • Claim to be medical. The disclaimer is on every screen that shows a score.
  • Record from a microphone. There is no getUserMedia anywhere.
  • Upload anything. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputNo machine name, or fewer than sixteen samples — refused with the field named.
No networkNothing changes; nothing is uploaded.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the recordings on screen are unchanged.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty history.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js.
