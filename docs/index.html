What it does

Takes a sample buffer, computes a small spectrum, and keeps the first recording as the machine's own baseline. A later recording is scored band by band, and whatever stands out is named.

What it refuses

It will not diagnose: there is no condition, fault or verdict field in the engine. A microphone is not built either — the app works on a buffer it can hold and a test can check.
